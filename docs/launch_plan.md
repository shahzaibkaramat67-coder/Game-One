# Launch Plan — First 30 Days Targets

Goal: $10,000 revenue in first month via cosmetics + battle pass (no ads).

Key levers:
- Soft-launch UA ($3k) targeted to high-LTV geos
- Strong launch-week Founder's Pack limited offer
- Battle Pass priced at $4.99, aggressive placement in onboarding

Week 0: Finalize prototype and server stubs. (scaffolded)

Week 1: Core prototype playable locally; basic matchmaking integrated.

Week 2: Cosmetic store, sandbox Play Billing integration, analytics hooks.

Week 3: Soft-launch build + initial UA ($2-3k), Founder's Pack promo.

Week 4: Measure and iterate: optimize conversion funnels, add daily flash offers.

KPIs to monitor daily:
- Installs, DAU, D1/D7 retention, matches per DAU
- Conversion, ARPPU, ARPU, revenue per day

Immediate launch offers:
- Founder's Pack $9.99 for first 72 hours: premium pass + exclusive legendary skin
- Premium Battle Pass $4.99 (4-week season) with exclusive cosmetic
- Rotating $1.99 impulse skins in store
