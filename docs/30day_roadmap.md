# Week-by-Week Roadmap to $10k (30 Days)

## Week 1: Soft-Launch & Initial UA

**Goals:**
- 2k–5k installs
- Validate core loop & monetization
- Reach 2% conversion (test price points)

**Actions:**
1. Deploy production server + configure MongoDB
2. Build signed Android APK, upload to internal testing
3. Start $1k UA spend (Facebook + TikTok)
4. Launch Founder's Pack (72h limited)
5. Monitor crashes, D1 retention, conversion

**Metrics to hit:**
- D1 retention: >30%
- Conversion: >2%
- ARPPU: >$15
- Founder Pack sales: $1,500–$2,000

---

## Week 2: Closed Testing & Optimization

**Goals:**
- 5k–10k cumulative installs
- Expand beta testers to 200–500
- Refine pricing & offers based on week 1 data

**Actions:**
1. Analyze week 1 funnel: where do players drop?
   - High shop views but low purchases? Lower prices 10%
   - Low matches/DAU? Add daily rewards, improve tutorial
   - High crashes? Release hotfix, re-upload APK

2. Launch rotating cosmetic flash deals ($1.99 impulse skins)
3. Increase UA spend to $1.5k if ROAS >2:1
4. Add daily login bonus (week 1-2: 25 coins + cosmetic fragment)
5. Soft-launch referral system (both players get 50 coins for invite)

**Metrics:**
- DAU growth: +30% (from week 1 peak)
- Conversion trend: hold or improve
- ARPPU: $18–$25

---

## Week 3: Ramp UA + Mid-Season Content

**Goals:**
- 10k–20k cumulative installs
- 2.5–3% conversion
- Mid-season cosmetic refresh (new skins)

**Actions:**
1. Increase UA to $2k/week if ROAS holding
2. A/B test: offer variant A (premium pass only) vs B (premium + 100 coins bundle)
3. Launch **mid-season cosmetic drop** (new skins + emotes)
4. Add weekend events: 2x XP Friday–Sunday
5. Early leaderboard "pre-season" — seed top 100 with influencers/testers

**Metrics:**
- Average weekly spend: $500–$750 UA
- ARPPU tracking: $20–$30
- Cumulative revenue run-rate: $3,000–$5,000

---

## Week 4: Full Launch Prep + Scaling

**Goals:**
- 20k–40k cumulative installs
- Prove $10k revenue feasible
- Prepare for global launch (or scale soft-launch)

**Actions:**
1. If performance strong (>$5k revenue, >2% conversion):
   - Increase UA to $3k/week (aim for 10k–15k new installs/week)
   - Prepare global launch submission (all language variants, age rating, etc.)

2. If performance weak (<$5k revenue, <1.5% conversion):
   - Pause new UA, focus on retention
   - Price cosmetics lower by 20%
   - Boost login rewards (50 gems on day 7)
   - Cut Founder's Pack, run "value bundle" instead

3. Run end-of-season tournament (cosmetic prizes)
4. Tease Season 2 exclusive skins (unlock with battle pass)

**Metrics:**
- Week 4 goal revenue: $3,000–$5,000
- Cumulative month revenue: $7,000–$12,000
- D7 retention: 15–20% (healthy for PvP)

## Daily KPI Checklist

Every morning, check:
1. **Installs:** Trending up? Good. Flat? Increase UA spend.
2. **DAU:** Up from yesterday? Retention holding?
3. **Conversion:** Track cohort funnel (all users → shop viewers → payers)
4. **ARPPU:** Trending up? Your prices are optimized. Down? Lower or add new cosmetics.
5. **Crash rate:** >0.5%? Emergency fix. <0.1%? Good.
6. **Match queue time:** >10s? Scale server. <3s? You're good.

---

## Pause/Pivot Signals

🚨 **PAUSE UA immediately if:**
- Crash rate >1%
- D1 retention <15%
- Negative ROAS (spending more than earning)

⚠️ **Slow down & iterate if:**
- Conversion <1% (prices too high, not enough exposure)
- ARPPU <$15 (players aren't finding value)
- Match queue >30s (server scaling needed)

🟢 **Scale aggressively if:**
- ROAS >3:1 (profitable UA)
- D1 retention >40% (sticky game)
- Conversion 4%+ (highly monetizable)
